# Brightwater Plumbing & Heating - Commercial Terms of Service

## Our charges

Every visit carries a call-out charge of £85.00, which includes the first 30 minutes on site. Labour after the first 30 minutes is charged at £60.00 per hour, in quarter-hour steps.

Work done out of hours is charged at 1.5 times the normal call-out charge and labour rate. Out of hours means before 08:00 or after 18:00 on weekdays, and any time at weekends or on bank holidays.

Parts are charged at cost plus 10%.

## Payment

Our invoices are payable within 30 days. Each invoice shows the job reference and the date and time of the visit.

## Cancellation

This is a call-off arrangement with no minimum term. Either side may end it at any time by email.

## Liability

Neither party limits its liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence. Otherwise, the total liability of Brightwater Plumbing & Heating under this agreement shall not exceed the charges paid in the preceding twelve months.

## Governing law

This agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction.
