Ember

An action game in the dark. Your flame is your life and your light: three acts, three bosses, nine ash-stones, and one question — who put out the Beacon?

Launch Ember → Engineering case study Source See its world in the atlas

The problem

A full action game, with lighting, bosses and a score, and not a single downloaded asset.

What it does

  • Three acts, three bosses and nine ash-stones
  • Lighting, shadows and score all generated in the browser
  • Keyboard, touch or gamepad

The most interesting decision

Fair by construction

Every attack is shown before it can land. The tests play the whole game on three seeds and assert that every hit was visible at least 0.3 seconds beforehand.

Built with

  • Canvas 2D lighting + shadows
  • Generated maps
  • Synthesised score
  • Keyboard, touch, gamepad

Evidence

Every room, every shadow and every note of the score is made in your browser. Read its tests

Its world

A dark rock with one beacon still burning. Embers rise from it and do not come down.
