The problem

Regular expressions fail invisibly, and sometimes they take a website down.

What it does

  • A pattern compiled into a machine you can watch run
  • Catastrophic backtracking made visible
  • Errors that point at the offending character

The most interesting decision

Every piece keeps its place in the pattern

Each node of the parsed pattern records its source offsets, so the trace can point back at a substring and an error can put a caret under the right character.

Built with

  • Parser
  • NFA compiler
  • Two matchers
  • Determiniser

Evidence

Catastrophic backtracking, visible. Read its tests

Its world

Stepping stones of a state machine, with a single character hopping between them.
