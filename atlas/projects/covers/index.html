Covers

A restaurant floor and booking system you drop into at 19:42 on a Saturday, mid-service, with a double-booking to sort out.

Launch Covers → Engineering case study Source See its world in the atlas

The problem

A Saturday night of tables, turn times and late parties is a puzzle solved in someone’s head.

What it does

  • Drop a party on a table and it says whether they fit, and why not
  • Handles turn times, turnaround and joined tables
  • When the answer is no, it says what would work instead

The most interesting decision

The refusal is the product

The rules live in a module with no interface code in it, every question goes through them, and the screen never has an opinion of its own about the room. When the answer is no, it says why and what to do instead.

Built with

  • Constraint-checked scheduling
  • Joined tables + turnaround
  • Drag and drop
  • Explains every refusal

Evidence

Drop a party on a table and it says whether they fit, and if not, why and what to do instead. Read its tests

Its world

A floor plan floating in the night, mid-service, with guests moving between the tables.
