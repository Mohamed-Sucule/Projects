Split

Who paid for what on a trip or at dinner, and the fewest bank transfers that settle everybody up, to the penny.

Launch Split ↗ Engineering case study Source

The problem

Groups settle up with far more bank transfers than they need.

Evidence

Settles everybody to the penny in the fewest transfers. Read its tests

What it does

  • Who paid for what, and who owes whom
  • The fewest bank transfers that settle everyone
  • Share the group by link; nothing is uploaded

The most interesting decision

Money is whole cents

Every amount is an integer from the moment it is typed, so every split sums to its total and every group balances to exactly zero, checked on thousands of random groups.

Built with

  • Minimum-transfer settlement
  • Exact money arithmetic
  • Share by link, no server
