Tether

You swing round a planet on a tether. Tap and you let go, flying off exactly the way you were already pointing.

Launch Tether → Engineering case study Source See its world in the atlas

The problem

A whole game out of one decision: when to let go.

What it does

  • Swing round a planet and tap to let go along the tangent
  • Every part of the decision is drawn on screen
  • Points for people who want more than survival

The most interesting decision

Every part of the decision is drawn

The tangent, a short aim line and each planet’s capture radius are all visible, so no death is arguable.

Built with

  • Orbital physics
  • Canvas 2D
  • Web Audio

Evidence

Let go and you fly off along the tangent, exactly as fast as you were going. Read its tests

Its world

A tiny planet, and something swinging round it on a line.
