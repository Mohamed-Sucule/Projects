Call Coach

A live assistant for sales calls: the right answer on screen the moment a prospect objects, and the notes, CRM record and follow-up written when the call ends.

Launch Call Coach ↗ Engineering case study Source

The problem

Reps lose deals on the same objections, and nobody writes the call up properly afterwards.

Evidence

27 of 28 new lines tagged exactly right, first time; every quote in its notes checked against the call. Read its tests

What it does

  • Puts the right battlecard on screen the moment a prospect objects
  • Writes MEDDICC notes, a CRM record and the follow-up email when the call ends
  • Nudges the rep when they pitch before finding a problem or do most of the talking

The most interesting decision

Every quote in the notes was actually said

The notes and the follow-up quote the prospect’s own words, and a test checks every quoted line against the call, so the summary cannot put words in anyone’s mouth.

Where the AI is, and where it isn’t

The model

  • None. Every tag, card, nudge and note comes from rules a sales manager would recognise, so each one can be read and tested

Ordinary code

  • Tags seventeen kinds of moment: eight objections, five buying signals and four pains
  • Reads spoken numbers and sums of money into values with their units
  • Writes MEDDICC notes, a CRM record and the follow-up email from quotes

What keeps it honest

  • A test checks that every quote in the notes and the email was actually said on the call
  • Held-out lines, run once: 27 of 28, precision 96%, recall 100%
  • The same person wrote the rules and the test lines, so that score is optimistic, and the page says so

Not built yet

  • No call audio yet: it needs a speech-to-text service with speaker labels
  • CRM records are drafts for the rep to approve

Built with

  • Moment tagger: 17 kinds
  • Spoken-number parser
  • MEDDICC notes
  • CRM record + follow-up
  • Quote checking
