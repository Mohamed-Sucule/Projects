The problem

Two players on one keyboard, one key each, and it still has to be fair.

What it does

  • Two players, one keyboard, one key each
  • A ring that closes while you fight
  • A bot when you are on your own

The most interesting decision

Tuned by measurement, not feel

Every rule was chosen by having bots play thousands of rounds. The first draft let the closing ring decide 91% of rounds; the tuned rules make the shoves decide them.

Built with

  • Canvas 2D
  • Web Audio
  • A bot for one player
