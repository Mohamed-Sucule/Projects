        Sales AI · Revenue teams
        
    
        The right answer on screen the moment a prospect objects.
        
    
        A live assistant for sales calls. It tells the rep when they're talking too much, and when the call ends it writes the notes, fills in the CRM and drafts the follow-up.
        
    
          
      
        • The right card, right thenPrice, a competitor, "I'd need to ask my boss": each brings up what it usually means, the question to ask back and one way to answer.
          • 
      
        • Notes from what was saidEvery line in the notes is a quote from the call or a number read off one. Anything the call didn't cover is marked as the question to ask next time.
          • 
      
        • Honest about bad callsA polite "just send me an email" is written up as a polite no, with the one thing still worth doing.
          • 
    
        

    
        
      
        How it works
        
      
          
        
        1. Hears each line
          On a real call, speech-to-text turns each speaker's audio into lines as they finish speaking. Here the lines are written out, and the same code reads them.
          2. 
        
        2. Tags the moment
          Seventeen kinds of moment (eight objections, five buying signals, four pains), each a set of patterns a sales manager would recognise, checked against labelled examples.
          3. 
        
        3. Coaches, once
          Each card appears the first time its moment comes up, never twice. Nudges watch talk time, questions asked, and whether a buying signal was followed by a next step.
          4. 
        
        4. Writes it up
          The notes, the CRM record and the email are built only from what was said: quotes, numbers read off quotes, and the gaps as questions.

        precision and recall on the held-out set: how often a card was right, and how many moments it caught.
        
      
        
      
        Read the held-out number with a pinch of salt: the rules and the test lines were written by the same person, and people are bad at writing tests that surprise their own rules. The real test for a tagger like this is a week of a client's own call transcripts, the first thing I would ask for.
        
    
        

    
        
      
        Taking it to a real sales team
        
      
        
        
        The call audio next step
        Zoom, Teams and dialler recordings stream to a speech-to-text service with speaker labels. Everything on this page already works one line at a time.
        
        
        Their playbook designed for
        The cards, competitors and price are one file their best rep can edit. The tagger's patterns grow from their own transcripts, with every change scored against the labelled set.
        
        
        Their CRM next step
        The record and the notes map straight onto Salesforce or HubSpot fields, written as a draft the rep approves, never updated behind their back.
        
        
        Coaching the coach built
        Each tag is scored on its own for precision and recall, so "the price card fires too often" is a number that can be watched, not a feeling.
