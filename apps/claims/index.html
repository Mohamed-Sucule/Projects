Document AI · Insurance

    
An insurance claim, from paperwork to a decision.

    
A claim arrives as an email, a scanned claim form and an invoice. The desk reads all three, checks them against the policy and against each other, and recommends a decision, with the evidence for every line. A person approves it.

    
    
      
  • Reads the scans itselfText recognition runs on your own device. Watch it read the form; nothing is uploaded anywhere.
    • 
      
  • Every line has evidenceEach check points at a field on a document or a clause of the policy. Click it and the page shows you where.
    • 
      
  • A person decidesIt recommends. When a scan is hard to read, it sends the claim to a person rather than guessing.

How it works

      
    
        
  1. Reads
    Tesseract, an open-source text recogniser, reads the scanned form and invoice in your browser. Every word comes back with a position, so every field can be boxed on the page.
    2. 
        
  2. Understands
    Labels are matched loosely and values are cleaned for what they should be (£ read as E, O read as 0, a comma read as a full stop), so a smudge doesn't become a wrong number.
    3. 
        
  3. Checks
    Is the policy in force? Is it covered? Do the email, the form and the invoice agree? Does the invoice add up? Anything unusual, like a receipt dated after a theft, is flagged.
    4. 
        
  4. Recommends
    Approve, decline with the clause, or refer to a person, with the payout worked out and the reply drafted. The claims handler approves or overrides, and that goes on the record.
    5. 
      

    


    

      
Taking it to a real insurer

      

        
Their inbox and systems next step
Claims arrive from the claims inbox and the portal; the policy comes from the policy administration system, not a file. The checks don't change.

        
Their wording designed for
Every rule cites a clause by name, so changing a policy wording is editing a clause, and every recommendation says which clause it rested on.

        
Safe when unsure built
Tested against thousands of deliberately smudged scans: a misread can send a claim to a person, and never pays a claim it shouldn't or declines one it shouldn't.

        
A record of who decided built
Recommendation, approval and any override are logged with the time and the reason: what a regulator, or the ombudsman, would ask to see.
